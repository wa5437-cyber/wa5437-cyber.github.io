SheetScope

An app that turns a sheet-music PDF or photo into an editable score, and a MIDI file into sheet music. Results are a first draft to check and correct in the editor.

  • Turn sheet-music PDFs, photos or camera scans into an editable score (a first draft, for reference) (free: first page; all pages: Pro)
  • Turn MIDI files into sheet music, with no bar limit (quantised MIDI)
  • Fix pitches, durations and accidentals, and listen right away
  • Export to MusicXML, MIDI and PDF (Pro)
  • Recognition, editing and export all on your device, no account, no ads
App StoreFree to start · Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase (not a subscription)

Reads piano grand staff too

It reads lead sheets, single-line melodies, piano grand staff and drum notation. Complex piano pieces and drums are less accurate, so treat them as a guide. The screen shows a complex piece.

MIDI file to sheet music

MIDI files exported from a DAW become sheet music with no bar limit. Quantised MIDI is exact; performed (unquantised) MIDI like the example shown here, and three or more voices, are for reference only.

SATB choir systems

SATB choir scores are recognised, and you can select notes in the editor and move them up or down to correct the result.

How to use

  1. Bring in a score
    Add a PDF, photo or camera scan, or a MIDI file exported from your DAW.
  2. Check and correct the draft
    Fix pitches, durations and accidentals in the draft, and play it back to listen.
  3. Export
    With Pro, export MusicXML, MIDI or PDF and open it in MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale and others.
