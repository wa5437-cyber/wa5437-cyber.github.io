Yul — Gugak Tuner & Jangdan

A tuner and rhythm trainer for Korean traditional instruments, with a yul-name tuner, jangdan on a jeongganbo grid, and theory articles in one app.

  • A tuner that shows the nearest yul name (黃, 太, 仲 …) and the deviation in cents
  • Reference tones from real daegeum and piri recorded by the National Gugak Center
  • Dozens of court, folk, pungmul and gut rhythms played on a jeongganbo grid
  • Chain rhythms together and tap cells to make your own pattern
  • Theory articles in Korean, English and Japanese, jangdan explanation cards, and recording, sigimsae and practice (one-time purchase)

Jangdan you can see and hear

The current cell follows along as it plays. Change the BPM or tap cells to make your own pattern.

Chain rhythms together

Play several jangdan in sequence, such as the Sanjo chain from jinyangjo through jungmori, jungjungmori and jajinmori to hwimori.

Theory articles with illustrations

Read about the twelve yul, jo and tori, sigimsae, instruments and tuning, and how to read jeongganbo, in Korean, English and Japanese.

How to use

  1. Play a note into the tuner
    The nearest yul name and the deviation in cents appear. Set hwangjong to the court-music standard (Eb) or to any Hz you type.
  2. Tune against the reference tone
    Listen to the daegeum or piri reference recorded by the National Gugak Center, and tune gayageum, geomungo, haegeum and ajaeng string by string.
  3. Pick a jangdan and play it
    Choose a rhythm in the Jangdan tab, set the BPM and follow the jeongganbo grid.
  4. Read the theory behind it
    Use the Theory tab and each jangdan's explanation card to learn yul, jo, sigimsae and how to read jeongganbo.
