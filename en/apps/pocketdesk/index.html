PocketDesk — Your Mac, from your iPhone or iPad

A remote app that shows your Mac screen on your iPhone or iPad over the same Wi-Fi, so you can click, scroll and type. Devices connect directly, with no account and no server.

  • Aim and tap to click, drag, right-click and two-finger scroll
  • Type letters, numbers and symbols plus Return, Tab, Esc and arrow keys
  • Devices connect directly on your Wi-Fi, encrypted, and only registered devices can join
  • Pro: hear your Mac's sound, Apple Pencil, modifier keys (⌘⌥⌃⇧) and F1–F12
  • Pro: trackpad mode, up to 8x zoom, and up to your Mac's resolution (4K max)
App StorePro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) · free to start with Lite

Stays on your own Wi-Fi

The Mac helper and your device connect directly on the same network. There is no account to create and nothing passes through our servers.

Pick a Mac to connect

Register once at the Mac with a QR code or a number. You can remove registered devices from the helper at any time.

See, tap and type

Turn sideways for a wider view and type with the on-screen or a Bluetooth keyboard. Sound arrives slightly late, so it suits listening, not syncing to video.

How to use

  1. Install the helper on your Mac
    Get the free, notarized helper at software.mlmusicstudio.co.kr/pocketdesk/download/.
  2. Allow permissions once
    When the helper asks, allow Screen Recording and Accessibility.
  3. Register your device
    Scan the QR code or enter the number shown by the helper.
  4. Tap your Mac in the app
    Open PocketDesk and choose your Mac to connect.
  • iOS and iPadOS 17 or later
  • Free Mac helper (notarized by Apple)
  • No account, no server
  • Pro is one-time, not a subscription
