AET Quiz

A quiz app for practicing audio engineering calculations, from Ohm's Law to RT60, with randomly generated questions.

  • Four categories (electrical, delay, acoustics, tempo & pitch) plus mixed questions
  • Choose 5, 10, 15 or 20 questions; a fresh random set every time
  • Instant explanation with the formula after each answer
  • Live round progress, hit count and accuracy
  • No login, no ads, no tracking; works offline
App StoreFree · no ads, no login

Random calculation questions

Questions on electrical, delay, acoustics and tempo topics, such as speed of sound by temperature, the period of a frequency, and playback speed and pitch, are generated fresh each time.

A formula explanation for every answer

Pick an answer and the correct choice is highlighted together with the formula and the working.

Round result and accuracy

At the end of a round you see your score and accuracy, and can start another round right away.

How to use

  1. Pick a category
    Choose electrical, delay, acoustics, tempo & pitch, or mixed.
  2. Set the number of questions
    Select 5, 10, 15 or 20 and start.
  3. Answer and read the explanation
    Each answer comes with the formula and working.
  4. Check your result and go again
    See your accuracy, then try a new random set.
