AudioEngineerToolkit

A set of offline calculators for audio engineers, ready on stage or in the studio with no internet.

  • Electrical: Ohm's law, power, speaker impedance (series/parallel), dB ↔ power ratio
  • Delay: distance ↔ time ↔ phase, Haas effect zone finder, multi-zone speaker delay alignment
  • Acoustics: frequency and wavelength, octave bands, room volume, room modes, RT60
  • Tempo & Pitch: tap tempo, note length, playback speed ↔ pitch, loop BPM, latency calculator
  • Keep your go-to calculators in the Favorites tab and open them in one tap

How to use

  1. Pick a category
    Open Acoustics, Electrical, Delay or Tempo & Pitch from the bottom tabs.
  2. Enter your values
    Type in the numbers you have and the result is calculated right away.
  3. Save favorites
    Star the calculators you use most to keep them in the Favorites tab.
