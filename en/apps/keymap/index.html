DAW Shortcuts — KeyMap

An app to search, translate and drill keyboard shortcuts across six DAWs: Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Studio One and Cubase.

  • Search shortcuts for six DAWs by name, action or key, in 8 languages
  • Shows every key bound to a command, with Mac or Windows key labels
  • Translate a shortcut from one DAW to the same action in the other five
  • Spaced-repetition drills: flashcards and multiple choice, practice sets by category, daily streak
  • Works fully offline, no account required
App StoreFree · Pro is a one-time unlock, no subscription

Search by name, action or key

Find any of thousands of shortcuts by name, action or key, or browse by category and by DAW.

Spaced-repetition training

Flashcards ask you to recall first, then self-check; multiple choice tests you against plausible wrong keys. Items you keep missing come back.

Your main DAW is free

All shortcuts for the DAW you pick are free. Pro unlocks all six DAWs, cross-DAW translation and unlimited training in one purchase.

How to use

  1. Pick your main DAW
    On first launch choose one of the six DAWs. All its shortcuts are free.
  2. Search or browse shortcuts
    Search by name, action or key, or browse by category and by DAW.
  3. See the key in other DAWs
    One tap shows what the same action is called in other DAWs (cross-DAW translation is Pro).
  4. Train until it sticks
    Practice with flashcards or multiple choice and keep a daily streak.
