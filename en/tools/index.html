Free web tools

Audio tools you can use in the browser with no install. The apps with the same names go further.

Measurement & signals

  • RTA real-time analyzer

    Shows the sound coming into your device microphone as a 31-band (ISO 266) spectrum in real time.

  • Signal generator

    Pink and white noise, sine, square and triangle waves, hum, buzz and hiss for checking speakers and signal chains.

Ear training

  • 31-band ear trainer

    Train your ear to hear which frequency band was boosted or cut in pink noise — with your own music too.

  • Effects listening

    Compare reverb, delay, chorus and more, plus compressor, gate and ducking, with the effect on and off.

Calculators

  • Delay calculator

    Convert delay time, distance, frequency and phase, and check speaker alignment.

  • Acoustics calculator

    Frequency, wavelength and period, notes, room modes and reverb time (RT60).

  • Electrical calculator

    Ohm's law, power, impedance, dB-to-power conversion and cable voltage drop.

  • Tempo & pitch calculator

    Tap tempo, note lengths per BPM, speed and pitch, and loop BPM.

Exam

  • Stage-sound certification quiz (Korean, level 3)

    Multiple-choice questions from 14 chapters of the standard textbook, plus calculation problems. (Korean only.)
