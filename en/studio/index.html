ML Music Studio Recording

Recording, mixing and mastering at a studio in Yongsan, Seoul.

What we do

Recording

We record instruments and vocals.

Mixing

We bring the recorded sounds together into one song and balance them.

Mastering

We finish the mix so that it is ready to be released as a track.

Stage sound

We handle sound at live performances and events.

How it works

  1. Inquiry
    Contact us through the KakaoTalk open chat or by email.
  2. Consultation
    We sort out the work you want, the schedule and your reference tracks together.
  3. Work
    We carry out the work as agreed.
  4. Delivery
    We hand over the finished result.
