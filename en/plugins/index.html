OneKnob Saturator

A saturation plug-in for Mac. Pick tubes, iron, transistors or tape, then set it with one DRIVE knob. AU and VST3.

PlugSpot

Type a track and a plug-in, and it lands on that track. A Mac app for Pro Tools, Logic, REAPER and Ableton Live.

In development

MixTalk

In development. Say it, type it or touch it ("kick up 3 dB") and your live console follows, on iPad and Mac.

SessionBridge

In development. A Mac app that moves a DAW session to another DAW and gives you a report of what carried over and what you need to redo.
